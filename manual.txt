SECTION 1: GENERAL PUMP MAINTENANCE
Routine checks should be conducted every 500 operating hours. Inspect seals, housing, and alignment.

SECTION 2: VIBRATION & BEARING ANOMALIES
Fault Code E-202: High Radial Vibration (> 2.0 mm/s)
- Cause: Bearing race degradation, shaft misalignment, or unbalance in driven equipment.
- Immediate Action: Trigger scheduled shutdown within 2 hours if vibration exceeds 2.5 mm/s.
- Maintenance Procedure:
  1. Inspect drive shaft alignment using laser alignment tool. Tolerance must be within 0.05 mm.
  2. Perform bearing lubrication check. Replenish with Mobilith SHC 100 synthetic grease (15 grams).
  3. Inspect drive belt tension; adjust to 45-50 N.
