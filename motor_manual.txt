=== INDUSTRIAL MOTOR MODEL-X10 TECHNICAL MANUAL ===

SECTION 1: OVERHEATING & TEMPERATURE FAULTS
Fault Code E-101: High Stator Temperature (> 80°C)
- Cause: Insufficient airflow, cooling fan failure, or continuous overload operation.
- Immediate Action: Reduce load by 30% immediately. Inspect primary cooling intake for blockages.
- Maintenance Procedure: Clean air filters using compressed air (30 PSI max). Inspect cooling fan blades for cracks or mechanical obstruction.

SECTION 2: VIBRATION & BEARING ANOMALIES
Fault Code E-202: High Radial Vibration (> 2.0 mm/s)
- Cause: Bearing race degradation, shaft misalignment, or unbalance in driven equipment.
- Immediate Action: Trigger scheduled shutdown within 2 hours if vibration exceeds 2.5 mm/s.
- Maintenance Procedure: 
  1. Inspect drive shaft alignment using laser alignment tool. Tolerance must be within 0.05 mm.
  2. Perform bearing lubrication check. Replenish with Mobilith SHC 100 synthetic grease (15 grams).
  3. Inspect drive belt tension; adjust to 45-50 N.

SECTION 3: RPM & SPEED FLUCTUATIONS
Fault Code E-303: Speed Instability (+/- 50 RPM variation)
- Cause: Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) tuning parameters drift or feedback encoder fault.
- Maintenance Procedure: Recalibrate optical shaft encoder. Verify wiring continuity on VFD control terminal strip (Terminals 5 & 6).
